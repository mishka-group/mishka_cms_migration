Absolute permalink: the welcome post. Plain ID link: post by ID. Page ID link: page by ID.

Relative links: the team page and the blocks post. With an anchor: to a heading.

Media: the PDF, the hero attachment page, and a sized copy of the hero.

Archives: News, News by ID, the Elixir tag, Nadia.

Broken and external: a deleted post, elixir-lang.org, an email link.
